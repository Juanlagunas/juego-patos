# Personaje del juego

Creado con la herramienta integrada ImageGen a partir del dibujo aportado en la conversación. Se conserva el personaje humano, cabello largo, ojos grandes y postura. Viste un traje amarillo de pato y botas naranjas con pies de pato. La segunda edición añade uñas blancas en ambas manos.

## Prompt del diseño

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: transparent full-body character sprite for a children's beach puzzle game. Edit target: supplied pencil drawing. Keep the recognizable same anime-style girl, very long flowing hair with side bangs partially covering one eye, large expressive eyes, slender stylized drawing proportions, gentle tilted standing pose, outstretched arms, and tall boots. Change ONLY the outfit to an age-appropriate, fully covered, cute yellow duck costume: loose yellow short-sleeved tunic dress with a small duck-face emblem on the chest, yellow tights, orange tall boots styled like duck feet, and a tiny yellow duck cap perched on top of her hair, leaving face and long hair visible. She remains a HUMAN GIRL in a duck costume, with a human face, NOT a duck head or bird body. Give the original hair a soft brown color, eyes brown, outfit pastel yellow and orange. Clean polished 2D anime lineart faithful to the drawing, simple flat colors and subtle shading, crisp readable outline at game sprite scale. Entire single character fully visible with small padding, centered on a genuinely transparent background. No paper, no photo texture, no reference frame, no watermark, no text, no extra people, no objects. Preserve hairstyle, silhouette, face and pose as closely as possible.

## Prompt de la edición de uñas

Edit the supplied character illustration. Keep exactly the same girl, face, long brown hair, anime drawing style, body, pose, yellow duck costume dress, duck cap, orange tall boots, and colors. Add clearly visible pretty WHITE FINGERNAILS to the fingertips of BOTH outstretched hands, with modest rounded oval nails, anatomically attached to the fingers and visible against skin. Only change the fingernails. This is a game sprite: background must be completely transparent with no brown glow, no black background, no scenery or shadows behind the figure; preserve the whole full-body character including hair tips, hands and boots. No text or new objects.

## Prompt de las botas combinadas

Edit ONLY the boots of the supplied full-body character illustration. Combine both duck boot designs: keep the same tall knee-high shape and the wide three-toed webbed duck feet, but make the tall boot shafts bright pastel YELLOW, with a very clear cute DUCK FACE on the front of EACH boot (two dark round eyes and an orange bill). Keep the wide three-toed duck-foot TOE and SOLE sections ORANGE. The combination should unmistakably be tall yellow duck-face boots with orange webbed duck feet. Keep everything else exactly the same: human girl, face, huge eyes, very long brown hair, tilted pose, outstretched hands with WHITE FINGERNAILS, yellow duck dress, duck cap, yellow tights, drawing style and framing. Transparent background, no new backdrop. Entire full body visible, no text.
